# Homepage override

This page follows the user-approved direction and overrides the generated luxury defaults where noted.

## Visual direction

- Concept: **Clareza que sustenta**.
- Primary: `#081F35`; secondary navy: `#123954`.
- Background: `#F7F6F2`; foreground: `#14202B`.
- Accent: restrained grey-gold `#A49373`, limited to rules, labels, numbers, focus and primary CTA.
- Avoid black/gold luxury clichés, glossy liquid-glass cards and decorative blur.
- Use documentary project photography from the Randaza portfolio.

## Typography

- Editorial serif for display: Georgia / Times fallback, regular weight.
- Geist sans for navigation, UI labels and body copy.
- Large headings use sentence case, tight tracking and balanced wrapping.

## Layout and motion

- Spacious 12-column editorial grid with thin structural rules.
- Narrative sequence: positioning, expertise, projects, transparency, method, history, insights, contact.
- Native scroll; no scroll hijacking.
- Viewport reveals: 450-700ms, 12-20px movement, 40-80ms stagger.
- At most one visually pinned/sticky chapter on desktop; none on mobile.
- The page remains complete and readable without animation and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
